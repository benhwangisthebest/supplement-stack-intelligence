## Readings to check

- **berberine-metabolic, consistency 0, is the row that decides this batch.** The abstract gives only pooled estimates: no I², and no subgroup comparison. The convention therefore leaves consistency empty, and the effect derives **B (0.633)**. At consistency 1 it would be B (0.700). **At 2 it would be A (0.767).** A has no support in this abstract. U4 item 4 (*"comparable to first-line agents"*) goes to B5 regardless.
- **zinc-immune B → C (0.417).** The treatment benefit is a mean of −2.37 days at low certainty, with I² 97%. I scored effect size 2 on the magnitude alone. At 1 the composite is 0.367, still C.
